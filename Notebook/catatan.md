Business question
Indonesia
1. Apa alasan (reason code) yang paling sering menyebabkan retur produk?
2. Produk dan kategori produk apa yang paling sering diretur (berdasarkan frekuensi)?
3. Berapa total kerugian finansial akibat retur sepanjang tahun 2024, dan produk/kategori mana yang menyumbang kerugian terbesar?
4. Tipe instansi kesehatan (customer type) mana yang paling banyak melakukan retur?
5. Region mana yang mencatat jumlah kasus retur tertinggi?
6. Bagaimana tren retur sepanjang tahun 2024 — apakah ada pola bulanan atau musiman tertentu?

English
1. What are the most frequent reason codes for product returns?
2. Which products and product categories are returned most frequently?
3. What was the total financial loss from returns throughout 2024, and which products/categories contributed the most to that loss?
4. Which healthcare institution types (customer types) return products most often?
5. Which region recorded the highest number of return cases?
6. What was the return trend throughout 2024 — is there a monthly or seasonal pattern?
